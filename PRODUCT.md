# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sri Lankan university students and CodeSprint 11 participants (IIT + inter-university) buying limited-run event merch. They arrive from codesprint.lk or social posts, on phones as often as laptops. They already know the event; the page's job is to make the drop feel worth owning.

## Product Purpose

The official CodeSprint 11 merch storefront, run by the IEEE Student Branch of IIT. One collection (hoodie + tees), bank-transfer checkout, admin-managed inventory in Supabase. Success: the page feels like gaming/streetwear drop culture, not a student-club shop, and people screenshot it.

## Brand Personality

Engineered, nocturnal, limited. The voice of a hardware launch crossed with a streetwear drop: confident short sentences, technical labels, no exclamation marks.

## References

- rvlt-ranboo.basement.studio: viewport-scale display wordmark, asymmetric photo panels, marquee dividers with glyphs, serif italic inline accents.
- aexlab.com/vail-vr (+ /merch): products floating free on pure black (no card boxes), giant condensed product titles, tiny technical spec text, dot-grid void backgrounds.
- codesprint.lk: the parent brand. Pure black, orange #ff6a3d, EB Garamond + Rajdhani + Manrope.

## Anti-references

- Fake-status widgets: "Drop Status / LIVE NOW" pills, EQ bars, pulsing dots, stat rows (CS11 / LTD / IIT). The user explicitly called these "AI-like buttons".
- Icon + heading + text card trios. Numbered 01/02/03 spec card grids.
- Sample/stock 3D garments that aren't the actual merch.
- Generic SaaS landing grammar of any kind.

## Design Principles

1. The merch IS the imagery. Real product photos float on black; never decorate around a placeholder.
2. One dominant idea per fold. Long scroll, deliberate pacing, no section does two jobs.
3. Type carries the brand: Manrope extrabold uppercase for scale, EB Garamond italic for voice, Rajdhani for small technical labels only.
4. Orange is committed, not sprinkled: it owns whole bands (marquee, CTA) and otherwise stays scarce.
5. Motion is choreography, not garnish: scroll-driven reveals and parallax with reduced-motion fallbacks; nothing bounces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on black (body text ≥ rgba(255,255,255,0.7)); prefers-reduced-motion alternatives for every animation; product images carry descriptive alt text.
